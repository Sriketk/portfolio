There is this Tenet Radiohead reference that no one talks about.

Tenet is a movie by Christopher Nolan. The movie revolves around the idea of inversion. The characters are trying to go backwards in time as the rest of the world continues to live in regular forward-moving time.

Radiohead has a song called "Like Spinning Plates." It sounds super similar to the track "Foils" from the Tenet score.

<YouTube id="rbnlp8q3Rsk" start={5} caption="Ludwig Göransson — Foils (Tenet score)" />

<YouTube id="rYzIksH67hA" start={19} caption="Radiohead — Like Spinning Plates" />

At first, I thought it was a huge coincidence. But after some digging, I learned that Thom Yorke sang "Like Spinning Plates" in reverse and then played it backwards. A super similar theme to Tenet.
