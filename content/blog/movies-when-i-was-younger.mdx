## title: "Movies When I Was Younger" date: "2026-04-24" description: "Nostalgic reflections on the films that shaped my childhood" draft: false category: "Media"

When I was younger, it seemed like every movie I watched had a huge impression on me. I don't remember not liking a movie.

Going to the theater was a privilege for me. I thought about going to a movie the same way I would think about going to a theme park. I don't understand why I didn't beg my parents to watch more.
