When I was younger, it seemed like every movie I watched had a huge impression on me. There was no concept of good or bad. I say that cause I can't remember a movie I watched I didn't like. 

Watching a movie in the theaters was a genuine experience. I loved it, but for some reason I didnt watch that many. I think its cause I thought of it as something that you just dont do that often. Almost like it wasnt possible to do that. 

I would come back from the theaters constantly thinking about it. I would be excited to go to bed. 

This really has me thinking about my old house. I need to make sure we do not sell that house. That is the only resemblance of a home and my childhood that I have left. 

After I graduated highschool, my memories of these period often have to do with me thinking. But before highschool, my memories were actual moments.
