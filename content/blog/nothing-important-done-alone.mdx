When building Cobalt, I was super adamant on building alone and not raising any money. But now, I dont think I could be further than the opposite. 

If you can build something alone, you probably are not building something valuable enough. Sure you migh tme able to build small consumer apps, but for software that actually matters, there are no examples of people building it alone. 

My throughts on raising money has also changed. At the end of the day, its just monopoly money. Building a start up is a grind. If youre gonna do it, you might as well struggle with people and have fun doing it. Its monopoly money at the end of the day. 

My ideas of fulfillment has changed over the past few months. The only thing that matters is what you are building. Build something that actually matter with a team is better than building something trivial. It doesnt matter hwo much equity you have or wethe you would be on pace to exit and make money. the only thig that matters are the learnings and the people you are building it for.
