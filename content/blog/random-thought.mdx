# Random Thought

Sometimes the best ideas come when you're not actively looking for them. They arrive in the shower, during a walk, or right before you fall asleep. The trick isn't forcing creativity—it's creating space for it to emerge naturally.

I've noticed that the more I try to control my creative process, the more it resists me. But when I step back, take a break, and let my mind wander, that's when the breakthrough moments happen.

Maybe that's the secret: stop chasing, start allowing.
