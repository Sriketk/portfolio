We live at a time where anyone can build anything. It used to be the case that I was super particular about what I worked on cause the cycle for building and learning was so high. 

Now, with all of these costs gone, the only thing left is How hard am I willing to fight for. 

I started thinking about this the past month while building Cobalt. I am super proud of what I built and I am glad I built it, but I wasnt sure how much more I was willing to fight for it to succeed. I think part of it is cause I am building it solo. But I dont think I am willing to sacrifice my health in order for this to succeed. I am no longer building this product for myself anymore. I feel like I'm shilling features on a product that i do not even use myself. I dont know if I'm willing to sacrifice my 20's for this to succeed.
