Design is one of my biggest challenges in building software. For both frontend and backend. I didnt begin having much of a sense of design initially. But the thing with design is you know outright if something is good or bad. But the hard part is finding what exactly it is. And with design, its a sum of a bunch of small details. The text to width ratio, the alignment of icons. There is so much. And alot of it for me was just grunt work. I couldnt just this han this off ot AI. I probably spent hours looking at differnet buttons and cards to understand what makes good components. With design I coulndt paralleize and work at differnet design elements at the same time. I just needed to keep staring at small details. My lowest github commit weeks were probably the ones with desing work. I had to repeat this process over for so many other design elements like icons, fonts, and colors. You cant fake design as you can with engineering. With engineering, you can arrive at a solution by hacking a few parts together. With design, its either good or garbage.

For backend, I spent more time testign differnet shapes of the API's, file structures, and naming more than atually writing the code for it.
