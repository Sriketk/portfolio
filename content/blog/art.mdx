There seems to be a strong rift between artists and AI. With someone who has great appreciation for both, I dont understand the tension. 

The sole purpose of art is to elicit emotion and a medium for people to express themselves and resonate with. It does not matter how it is created.

Another common argument is about how companies are profiting on the work of artists. I partially agree with this, but I do not care about it that much. Artists rip off and sample each other's work already. At the end of the day, no artist is greater than art itself. Each artist is a culmination of the people before them. 

At the end of the day, you cant fake manufacture if people are going ot resonat with something. either they or they dont.
