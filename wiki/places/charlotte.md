The longest-lived setting of Sriket's life — home from fifth grade through high school, then again as the location of his first job and his solo-building stretch before NYC.

## Phases of Sriket's relationship to Charlotte

- **Settling in (5th–8th grade)**: arrival at the Camden Apartments, Hawker Ridge Elementary, then a new house and Elon Park Elementary. First place that didn't pull out from under him. See [[charlotte-era]].
- **High school**: competitive academic environment in Charlotte shaped his belief that the average person is only as smart as the people around them (see [[average-as-smart-as-peers]]).
- **Post-college**: returned to Charlotte to take the corporate job at Ally Financial. See [[ally-financial-era]].
- **Solo-builder stretch**: built [[cobalt]] from Charlotte for several months. The constraint that drove him out wasn't Charlotte itself but Charlotte's lack of startup community.
- **The departure**: see [[move-to-nyc]]. Charlotte stopped being a place where the next thing could happen.

## What Charlotte gave him

Stability for the first time. A competitive high school cohort. A family home for nearly a decade. A starting point.

## What Charlotte couldn't give him

A startup community. Events. Peers in tech who would compound his learning. This absence — not any defect of the city itself — is what made the [[move-to-nyc]] inevitable.
