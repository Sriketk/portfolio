The California stop in Sriket's nomadic childhood — fourth grade, the last move before Charlotte. The place he most associates with Pokémon.

## What it was

His father had a corporate job at Walmart. The family lived close to Mission Valley Elementary, where Sriket went. Sriket's younger twin brothers went to a Christian school called St. Dominicans.

## What was hard

Academically it was a challenging year for him. He's not sure whether the difficulty came from accumulated dislocation across the nomadic years (see [[childhood-nomadic]]) or from something else.

## The Pokémon era

Fremont is where Pokémon embedded itself. A Navy Blue DS Lite. Mario vs. Donkey Kong. Pokémon Black and White, with Snivy as his starter. Hunting Seven Sages via YouTube hacks. Heavy collecting — he still remembers his Greninja card. The closing image of the year: waking up his Russian neighbor early on the morning of his last day to trade Pokémon one final time.

## Sensory anchor

Oxalis flowers in the neighborhood — little sour edible blooms — are the smell-and-taste memory he carries from Fremont.

## What came next

Fifth grade brought Charlotte (see [[charlotte-era]] and [[charlotte]]), which ended the nomadic period.
