The specific Indian city — and neighborhood, Miyapur — where Sriket spent third grade and parts of preschool during his nomadic childhood. The site of his first vivid school memories.

## What it was

Extended family base. The family lived in the same building as Sriket's mother's older brother's family, downstairs. Cricket in the building's lobby is the sensory anchor of his memory of the place.

## School there

Third grade in India is where Sriket's first vivid school memories live. The discipline was physical and routinized: nail checks on Monday mornings, punishment laps if you failed them, knuckle strikes with metal rulers for wrong answers, ear-holding squats as classroom punishment, karate in gravel without shoes. He frames it now as "a very interesting experience" rather than as trauma.

## Family context

His father was working in the US through most of this period — eventually landing a job in California. The family didn't see him for roughly a year while Sriket was in third grade.

## What it became

Hyderabad shows up later as the site of the head-shaving ceremony Sriket was furious about in high school (see [[charlotte-era]]), and as the destination of the Uplift Humanity trip he took the summer between junior and senior year of high school. India is a recurring place in his life rather than a single chapter.
