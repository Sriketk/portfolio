The first decade of Sriket's life, spent moving between Houston, Florida, India, Detroit, and Fremont before settling in Charlotte. Defined by his father's job-driven relocations and Sriket's early exposure to radically different schooling systems.

## Origins

Sriket was born in Houston, Texas. His family stayed only six months before the first of many moves. His parents emigrated from small rural villages in India and were arranged-married, which Sriket frames as standard practice for their generation. His father attended the Indian Institute of Technology — Sriket calls IIT "the Harvard of India" — and was placed into food science. After struggling to find US work, his father came to the University of Massachusetts to study computer science while also doing a research assistantship in food science. His mother dropped her education to be a housewife — a sacrifice Sriket treats as the foundational debt of his life (see [[honor-the-sacrifice]]). Family lore from the UMass years centers on subsistence: a few hundred to a thousand dollars a month for groceries, long cold walks through Massachusetts winters. Sriket describes his father as "the most hard-working person I know" — a man who knew nothing but work and spent hours daily at his desk.

## Route

After Houston came Florida, then India for preschool, then Detroit for second grade, then back to India for third grade, then Fremont, California for fourth grade. The cadence of the moves shaped his early schooling more than any single place did.

## Detroit (second grade)

Brief and warmly remembered. Kroger's runs and the birth of his younger twin brothers.

## India (third grade, Miyapur, Hyderabad)

His first vivid school memories. Discipline was physical: teachers checked nails on Monday mornings; missed cuts meant running campus laps. Wrong answers in class were punished by holding ears, doing squats, or taking metal-ruler knuckle strikes. Karate class happened in gravel without shoes. The family stayed near his mother's older brother's family — cricket games in the building's lobby were a fixture. His father remained in the US working, eventually landing a job in California; the family didn't see him for roughly a year.

## Fremont (fourth grade)

His father was working at Walmart corporate. They lived close to Mission Valley Elementary. This was a difficult academic year — Sriket attributes it to the cumulative dislocation. He got into Pokémon (Navy Blue DS Lite, Mario vs. Donkey Kong, black-and-white Pokémon with Snivy as starter, hunting Seven Sages via YouTube hacks). He collected cards heavily — his Greninja stands out in memory. His younger twin brothers attended a Christian school called St. Dominicans. A Russian neighbor became his close friend; the night before leaving California, Sriket woke him up early to trade Pokémon. Edible oxalis flowers from the neighborhood are a sensory anchor of the year.

## What the era shaped

The moving stopped at the next stop — [[charlotte-era]]. The cumulative effect was that Sriket entered Charlotte without the social baseline most kids start school with, which would echo through middle school and shape his later belief that [[average-as-smart-as-peers]].
