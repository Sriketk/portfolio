Sriket's life in New York City beginning January 2026, after moving from Charlotte. He lives in Woodside, Queens for around $1,050 in rent. The era is defined by access to a real tech community, an obsession with sync engines and fast web apps, a full rewrite of [[cobalt]], and a shift in intent toward joining a team rather than continuing to build alone.

## The move

Sriket moved to New York in January 2026 as a deliberate "fuck it" act — see [[move-to-nyc]]. The thesis was simple: if he wanted a better opportunity, it was better to be where opportunities live. Charlotte had given him no startup community, no events, no one to really talk to. NYC (or SF) was the bet.

## Where he lives

Woodside, Queens. Rent around $1,050/month.

## Bootstrap stance

Consuming more tech Twitter through the [[solo-builder-era]] left Sriket tired of online glorification of fundraising over building. He chose deliberately to bootstrap [[cobalt]] — "you probably shouldn't raise money if you don't need to." The stance is partly philosophical (build a real business organically) and partly defensive (he heard too many horror stories). See [[bootstrap-philosophy]].

## Sync engines obsession

In New York, Sriket discovered sync engines and became obsessed. The interest crystallized around the idea of a website with no loading states — every click renders data instantly. He found this "the coolest thing ever." He went deep on Zero, the sync engine built by the Replicache team. Nothing else he had seen worked like it.

The obsession had product consequences: he rewrote the entire Cobalt app from Next.js into TanStack to enable client-side server routing with Zero, paired with Hono. See [[cobalt]].

## Where it's headed

By June 2026, the era is shifting in intent. Sriket has spent the prior eight months building alone (see [[solo-builder-era]]) and has concluded that great things are never done alone (see [[great-things-not-done-alone]]). He is actively looking to join a team and work on something impactful. The solo grind era is closing; the next chapter is collaborative.
