Sriket's undergraduate years at the University of Maryland, College Park, where he studied computer science. Defined less by academics than by COVID isolation, a basketball obsession, and a near-absence of any career ambition beyond landing a normal corporate job.

## Why Maryland

The choice was somewhat accidental. Sriket applied as a pre-med through most of his senior year of high school. Late in the cycle he pivoted to computer science (see [[switch-to-cs]]). Most of his applications were locked into medical-leaning schools by then. Maryland College Park offered a free application, so he applied on that basis. It turned out to be the best computer science school he was admitted to.

## Freshman year (COVID)

His freshman year was a COVID year. Meeting people was hard: he didn't know anyone coming in from out of state, and dorm rules forbade visiting other students' rooms. He got into movies during this period.

## Basketball

The single most important thread of his college life. The university let students book a half court alone to shoot around — Sriket took three-hour sessions daily on top of lifting every day. He was also vegetarian and the dining options were limited. He calls this his "peak physical shape."

He had no academic passion. The only thing he genuinely cared about was getting better at basketball. Open runs are his strongest college memories, and they were how he made all of his friends. He suspects, in retrospect, that his pursuit of basketball improvement was downstream of wanting to make and deepen friendships rather than a love of the sport in isolation.

## Career outlook (such as it was)

Sriket gave very little thought to his career during college. The plan was modest: get an internship, land an entry-level corporate job after graduation, be content. There was no startup ambition yet — that would arrive years later during [[ally-financial-era]].

## Exit

After college he took the entry-level corporate job he had aimed for — at Ally Financial back in Charlotte (see [[ally-financial-era]]).
