In January 2026, after roughly six to eight months of building solo in Charlotte during [[solo-builder-era]], Sriket moved to Woodside, Queens. The decision was framed as a "fuck it" move toward opportunity density.

## The case for going

Charlotte during the solo period offered:
- No startup community
- No events
- No real peers to talk to
- A daily loop of apartment work and the gym, with no inputs from outside himself

NYC (or SF) offered the inverse — a dense tech community, regular events, peers, ambient input. Sriket's framing: if he wanted a better opportunity it was just better to live where opportunities are.

## The path leading in

The move was preceded by:
- The one-week NYC scouting trip taken before quitting Ally (see [[quit-ally]]).
- A growing sense during [[solo-builder-era]] that solo-in-Charlotte had a ceiling.
- A pre-existing love of New York City from earlier trips.

## What the move enabled

- Exposure to the sync-engine community and the discovery of Zero/Replicache (see [[queens-era]]).
- The shift in self-understanding that opens the next chapter: that great things are never done alone (see [[great-things-not-done-alone]]), and the intent to join a team.

## What it cost

Rent of roughly $1,050/month in Woodside is the explicit number from the dump. The non-monetary cost — leaving the family base in Charlotte for the first sustained period — is not deeply explored in the raw material.
