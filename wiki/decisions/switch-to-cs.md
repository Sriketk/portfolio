Senior year of high school, partway through AP Biology, Sriket abandoned a pre-med trajectory and pivoted to computer science. The pivot was late enough that most of his already-submitted college applications still skewed medical, which constrained where he could go.

## The trigger

He took AP Bio (or at least half of it) and the interest he'd assumed he had in medicine collapsed against the material. The switch to CS was less a positive pull than a negative push: he no longer wanted what he'd been pointing at.

## The cost of being late

By the time he switched, his applications were locked in around medical-leaning schools. He frames this as the unfortunate timing of the decision — he didn't get to apply to schools picked for CS.

## How Maryland happened

University of Maryland College Park offered a free application. Sriket signed up and applied on that basis alone. It happened to be the best CS school he was admitted to, which is the only reason he ended up there — see [[maryland-college]]. The decision and the Maryland outcome are tightly bound: had Maryland not been a free, frictionless application, the CS pivot might have left him with no good CS school to go to.

## What it changed

Without this decision there is no [[ally-financial-era]], no software career, no [[cobalt]]. It is the foundational career pivot — late, half-accidental, and load-bearing.
