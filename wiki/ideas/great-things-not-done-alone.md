A belief Sriket arrived at empirically after eight months of solo building during [[solo-builder-era]]. The claim, in its sharpest form: anything impactful is always built with a team, and thinking otherwise is egotistical.

## Origin

The belief is the post-hoc summary of a lived eight months. Sriket spent that stretch building [[cobalt]] alone in Charlotte, then NYC. He shipped a real product, learned the full stack, did the design work, handled the infrastructure. By the end he framed it directly: he had spent the past eight months building alone, was now looking to join a team, and truly believed that great things are never done alone. He's explicit that he learned this the hard way — by trying the alternative and hitting its ceiling.

## The sharper framing

The version he committed to as a life principle is harder than gratitude or preference: "Anything impactful is always built with a team. Thinking otherwise is egotistical."

The word *egotistical* does the work here. It reframes the lone-founder posture from "ambitious" to "self-aggrandizing." The belief isn't "teams are nice" — it's "the belief that you can do it alone is an ego problem."

## What he's pulled toward

- Joining a team.
- Working on something impactful.
- Collaborative work, in contrast to the solo grind.

## What it implies he's leaving

- The lone-founder identity that animated [[solo-builder-era]].
- The bootstrap-by-default stance attached to [[bootstrap-philosophy]] — though it's unclear whether the bootstrap stance survives the team shift or is recontextualized.

## Why it lands now

The belief lands now and not earlier because Sriket needed direct experience to know it. Reading it as advice from someone else wouldn't have worked — he'd have dismissed it. Living it produced the conviction.

## Status

This is a current, forward-looking belief as of June 2026. It is shaping his next moves more than any other idea in the wiki.
